AI Prompts for Marketing

Two prompts for the top of the funnel: know what your competitors are paying for, and never run out of content. Fill in the brackets and let the AI ask you the rest.

Written by Bruno Ashford, Founder and CEOAI strategist, 10+ years running businesses and building agents for owners · every prompt here is one I use in real client work · Updated Sep 11, 2026
← All prompt categories

See what competitors are paying to advertise

Claude Code
Research the Google Ads and keywords my top competitors are bidding on for [my service] in [my city or region]. Show me the terms, roughly how competitive each one is, and which ones they're winning that I'm not showing up for. Then tell me the three keywords I should go after first and why.
  1. Replace [my service] and [my city or region] with your real details.
  2. Skim the terms it returns and note where competitors outrank you.
  3. Start with the three keywords it recommends first.

Turn one idea into a full week of content

Claude Code
Take this one idea and turn it into a week of content for [platform]: a scroll-stopping hook for each day, the angle, and the opening line. Make them sound like someone who actually knows the business, not a generic marketer. Ask me who the audience is before you write anything.
  1. Swap [platform] for where you post (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
  2. Tell it your audience and niche when it asks.
  3. Use each day's hook and opening line as the start of that post.

Frequently asked questions

Do these marketing prompts need any paid tools?

No. They work with the AI chat alone. If you connect Claude Code to real ad-data tools it gets sharper, but the prompt is useful on its own.

Will the content sound generic?

Only if you skip the questions. The prompts are written to make the AI ask about your audience and niche first, so the output sounds like your business.
