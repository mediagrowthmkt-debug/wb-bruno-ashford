What is an AI SEO agent?

An AI SEO agent is a specialist that runs inside your own AI assistant (Claude, Claude Code, Cursor or Codex) and does the work a senior SEO would: keyword research with real search volume, on-page audits, rank tracking, competitor gap analysis and full SEO reports. Meet Kevin, an AI SEO agent that ranks your site organically on Google.

Unlike a standard AI SEO tool or SaaS dashboard, Kevin is an installable agent. There is no separate app to open, no login and no monthly plan. You drop him into the assistant you already use and ask, in plain language, for a keyword research run, a technical audit or a rank check.

Kevin pulls real numbers (Google Search Console, GA4 and live SERP data via Serper) so recommendations are grounded in your actual data, not guesses.

Who Kevin is for and what he does

Kevin is built for founders, marketers, agencies and freelance SEOs who want an AI SEO tool that does the whole job: audit, build, publish and measure. If you already work in Claude, Cursor or Codex, he fits straight into that workflow.

Core skills: Keyword Research with real search volume and buyer intent, Position and Competitors (where you rank versus the leader), International Opportunities (low-competition markets to enter), a Full SEO Report combining GA4 plus Search Console plus Serper, Website SEO (audit, build, publish, measure) and Keyword Validation so the client signs off on the terms before you invest.

The outcome is simple: better organic Google rankings, more qualified traffic and an SEO process you can run on demand instead of paying for another rank tracking or keyword research tool every month.

How Kevin the AI SEO agent works

Ask in natural language. Kevin runs the keyword research, scores intent, audits the page against on-page best practices and checks Core Web Vitals, then hands you a prioritized plan with the copy and technical fixes spelled out.

Because he lives inside your assistant, Kevin reads your site, your Search Console and your competitors in one place. He tracks positions over time and tells you honestly whether a change moved the needle, using a 90-day SEO window rather than day-five panic.

No API key, no subscription and no black-hat shortcuts. Kevin owns the SEO end to end so you keep the authority you build.
