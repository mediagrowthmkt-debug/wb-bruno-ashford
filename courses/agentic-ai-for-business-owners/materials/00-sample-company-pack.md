# Sample Company Pack: Ashbrook Business Supply

Use this fictional company when you want to practise without touching your own data. Every exercise in the course works with it. The messy sales file in Module 3 (03-sample-messy-sales.csv) belongs to this company.

How to use it:
1. Create a folder called Ashbrook on your computer.
2. Save this file inside it as COMPANY.md.
3. Save 03-sample-messy-sales.csv inside a subfolder called sales.
4. Start Claude Code in the Ashbrook folder and treat it like a real business.

Nothing in this pack is real. Names, numbers and customers are invented for practice.

---

## 1. Who we are

- Name: Ashbrook Business Supply
- Founded: 2014
- Location: Leeds, UK, with a small sales office in Boston, Massachusetts
- Team: 14 people (owner, 3 sales reps, 2 customer service, 3 warehouse, 2 operations, 1 bookkeeper, 1 marketing coordinator, 1 part-time IT)
- Owner: Daniel Ashbrook, who still approves every quote above 5,000 and reads every complaint personally
- Annual revenue: about 2.4 million (GBP and USD combined)

## 2. What we sell

Three product lines to small and mid-sized businesses:

| Product line | What it is | Typical order | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumables | Paper, printer supplies, cleaning and clinical disposables | 150 to 900 | 22% |
| Equipment | Printers, shredders, dental and physio chairs, office furniture | 1,200 to 12,000 | 18% |
| Software | Resold licences for practice management and accounting tools, billed yearly | 600 to 4,800 | 35% |

Delivery: next working day for consumables in stock, 5 to 10 working days for equipment.

## 3. Who buys from us

Ideal customers are owner-run businesses with 5 to 60 staff that reorder every month:
- Dental and physio clinics (our biggest segment, about 38% of revenue)
- Trades and contractors (roofing, building, electrical)
- Professional services (accounting practices, law firms, agencies)
- Independent retail (home stores, gift shops)

Top accounts: Brightside Dental Clinic, Hartley Roofing Ltd, Oakwood Law LLP, Pinecrest Marketing Agency, Riverside Physio, Northfield Accounting, Greenleaf Home Store.

## 4. Sales team

| Rep | Territory | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Emma | Clinics and retail | Consumables and clinical equipment |
| James | Trades and accounting | Equipment and software |
| Olivia | Law firms and agencies | Software and office furniture |

## 5. Tone of voice

- Friendly, direct, practical. We sound like a helpful neighbour who knows their products, not a call centre.
- British spelling for UK customers, American spelling for US customers.
- Short sentences. No jargon. Always give a next step and a date.
- We never promise a delivery date the warehouse has not confirmed.
- We apologise once, clearly, then fix the problem.

## 6. Rules and red lines

- Never share one customer's prices or orders with another customer.
- Never send a quote above 5,000 without Daniel's approval.
- Never offer a discount above 10% without Daniel's approval.
- Refunds are processed only by the bookkeeper.
- Customer personal data (names, emails, phone numbers) stays inside our systems. Do not paste it into public tools.

## 7. How work gets done today (the pain)

| Task | Who | Hours per week | Why it hurts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly sales report for Daniel | Operations lead | 4 | Copying from three spreadsheets, formats never match |
| Chasing unpaid invoices | Bookkeeper | 5 | Same polite emails written by hand every week |
| Writing quotes | Sales reps | 9 (combined) | Each rep uses a different template |
| Answering "where is my order?" | Customer service | 7 | Looking up the same courier pages again and again |
| Following up old leads | Sales reps | 3 | Usually forgotten, so leads go cold |
| Monthly social posts and newsletter | Marketing coordinator | 6 | Starts from a blank page every month |
| Supplier price updates | Operations | 3 | Manual checks of supplier price lists |

Total: about 37 hours a week of repeatable work. At an average loaded cost of 28 per hour, that is roughly 1,036 per week, or about 53,900 per year.

## 8. Current problems Daniel wants solved this quarter

1. He wants a one-page Monday report without anyone spending half a day on it.
2. Overdue invoices are at 41,000 and growing.
3. Quotes take 2 days to go out; competitors reply the same day.
4. Two big clinic accounts complained about late deliveries last month.
5. The team is nervous that AI will replace them.
