## Monthly review agenda (45 minutes)

1. Actual against plan for each outcome (10 min).
2. Update the AI ROI Calculator and adoption scorecard (10 min).
3. Keep, fix or kill for every agent and routine (10 min).
4. Staying-current check: anything new that makes an existing workflow simpler, safer or cheaper? (10 min)
5. Adjust next month's outcomes, cut anything that no longer matters (5 min).
