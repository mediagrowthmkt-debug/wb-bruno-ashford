Scrubbing a two-hour episode for the three moments worth posting is the slowest part of podcasting. Podcast Clip Finder reads the full transcript and returns the strongest short clips and 8 to 10 minute YouTube blocks, each with a timestamp, a hook, a caption and a ranking. Built for podcast editors, creators and agencies who repurpose episodes at scale.

What you gain
  • A ranked shortlist of the best short clips for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, with exact in and out timestamps.
  • The strongest 8 to 10 minute continuous blocks for YouTube, each scored 0 to 10.
  • A hook and a ready-to-post caption for every clip.
  • The reason each pick will travel: story, authority, curiosity, hot take or emotion.
  • A closing call on the single strongest clip and block to publish first.
  • A self-contained review page to watch, rate 2 to 10 and approve every cut, hosted and saved wherever you choose.
How it works, step by step
  • 1
    Drop it into your AI

    Put the SKILL.md file in your skills folder (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) or paste it as a custom instruction. No app, no account, no API key, no install.

  • 2
    Paste your transcript

    Give it the full episode transcript, timestamps recommended. Nothing external to connect. If you forget it, the AI asks for it first, it never invents.

  • 3
    It reads the whole episode

    It reads everything before choosing. It selects and ranks, never summarizes, never invents a quote, and cuts only on clean sentence boundaries, never mid-word.

  • 4
    It finds the short clips

    Every standalone moment (story, authority insight, curiosity loop, hot take or emotion) becomes a 15 to 110 second clip, with the top 3 to 4 flagged as viral bets.

  • 5
    It finds the YouTube long blocks

    Continuous 8 to 10 minute windows that pass eight rules (one topic arc, strong hook, self-sufficient, full payoff inside, dense throughout, an emotional peak, a quotable line, monetization-safe), each scored 0 to 10.

  • 6
    You get a ready-to-cut shortlist

    Both lists with exact timestamps, hooks, captions and a ranking, plus a call on the single strongest clip and block to publish first, ready to hand to an editor.

  • 7
    It builds a review page

    A self-contained page (one HTML file) where you or your client watch each cut, rate it 2 to 10 and Approve, Request changes or Reject.

  • 8
    You choose where it lives

    It asks where to host the page and where to save the approvals, and wires itself to your setup, on an agentic AI it can even cut the clips with ffmpeg. Nothing hardcoded, no lock-in.

What it generates
  • Short-clip shortlist (15 to 110s): number, START to END, duration, subject, hook, caption, why it travels.
  • Top viral bets flagged (the 3 to 4 strongest short clips).
  • YouTube long-block list (8 to 10 min): number, window, duration, score /10, hook, 3 title options, thumbnail moment, mid-roll point, summary, risk flags.
  • A closing note: the single strongest clip, the strongest block, and what to publish first.
  • A self-contained review page (one HTML file): full episode on top, every cut with a player, a 2 to 10 rating and Approve / Request changes / Reject.
Variations

FAQ
What is a podcast clip finder?

A tool that scans a podcast transcript and surfaces the highest-potential short clips and long segments automatically, so you don't have to watch the whole episode.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It is a SKILL.md file you drop into your AI assistant (Claude, Cursor, Codex) and run. No app, no subscription.

How does it pick the best clips?

It analyzes hooks, story arcs and emotional peaks in the transcript, then ranks each moment by clip potential.

Can I use it for YouTube long cuts too?

Yes. It also flags 8 to 10 minute blocks ideal for a cuts channel, with titles and captions.
