Results ReportInstant download · license included · no subscription

Results Report is a marketing report skill you drop into your own AI assistant to turn raw numbers into a finished, on-brand PDF your client can actually read. Instead of copy-pasting metrics into slides, you point the skill at your results (social, SEO, ads, content, or whatever you track) and it builds a clean, sectioned client report with your logo, your brand colours, and plain-English commentary under each block. It is a practical alternative to bloated marketing reporting tools and dashboards: no login, no API key, no monthly fee, just a SKILL.md file that runs wherever your AI does. Great for agencies, freelancers, and in-house marketers who want automated reporting that still looks handmade.

What you gain
  • Hand clients a polished, on-brand PDF instead of a screenshot dump or a raw spreadsheet
  • Cut hours of manual reporting each month down to a single guided run
  • Keep every report consistent: your logo, your colours, the same clean layout every time
  • Explain the numbers in plain language under each section, so non-marketers understand the value
  • Stay honest and clear: dips are shown with context and next steps, not buried
  • Own your workflow with no subscription, no dashboard login, and no API key for the skill
How it works, step by step
  • 1
    Drop the skill into your AI assistant

    Add the SKILL.md file to Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no API key needed to use the skill.

  • 2
    Point it at your results

    Give it the marketing data you want in the report: metrics you export, numbers you paste, or figures from the tools you already use. You choose the reporting period, for example a specific month.

  • 3
    Set your brand once

    Tell it your logo, brand colour, and client name. It uses your original logo on a clean placard and matches the palette so the report looks made for that client.

  • 4
    It builds the report section by section

    It assembles only the sections that have real data (overview, social, SEO, content, ads, and more), each with a short, plain-English note underneath. Empty sections are simply left out.

  • 5
    It asks before anything it needs

    If it is missing a number, a logo, or a decision, it pauses and asks rather than inventing figures. You confirm the wording before it is finalised.

  • 6
    You get a finished PDF to review

    Out comes an on-brand PDF with a cover and closing page, ready to read. You approve the copy, and it stays frozen exactly as approved.

  • 7
    Share it with your client

    Send the PDF as your monthly or campaign report. Reuse the same setup next period for consistent, repeatable client reporting.

What it generates
  • An on-brand, client-ready PDF marketing report with your logo and colours
  • A branded cover page and closing page
  • Modular sections built only from data that exists (overview, social, SEO, content, ads, and more)
  • Plain-English commentary under each section for non-technical readers
  • A consistent, repeatable report layout you can reuse every reporting period
  • Editable section text you can approve or adjust before finalising
Variations

Monthly client report

Run it each month for a clean, on-brand recap that clients look forward to, with the same layout and your branding every time.

Focused report (only the sections you want)

Ask for just a subset, for example only social and SEO, and it builds a shorter report from those sections alone.

Campaign wrap-up

Point it at a single campaign or period to produce a tidy results summary you can hand off at the end of a project.

FAQ
What is the Results Report skill?

It is a marketing report skill: a SKILL.md file you add to your AI assistant that turns your marketing numbers into an on-brand, client-ready PDF, built one clean section at a time with your logo and colours.

Does it work with Claude, Cursor, and Codex?

Yes. It works with any AI assistant that reads a SKILL.md file, including Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.

Do I need to install anything or pay for an API key?

No. There is no separate app to install, no subscription, and no API key required to use the skill. You drop the SKILL.md file into your assistant and run it.

Do I need design or coding skills to use it?

No. You do not need design or coding skills. You give it your data, your logo, and your colours in plain words, and it handles the layout and formatting for you.

Will it make up numbers if data is missing?

No. If a metric, logo, or decision is missing, it asks you rather than inventing figures. Sections without real data are simply left out of the report.

Can I customise the branding and choose which sections appear?

Yes. You set the logo, brand colour, and client name, and you can ask for a full report or just specific sections such as social and SEO.

New to AI skills?

It runs in Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and any assistant that reads a SKILL.md or a system prompt. Because it is just text, it is fully portable, and if it needs something to run, results report asks you for it first instead of inventing.
