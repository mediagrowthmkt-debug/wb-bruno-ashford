Create PipelineInstant download · license included · no subscription

Create Pipeline is a downloadable AI skill that helps you build a sales pipeline the right way: clear, ordered CRM pipeline stages that mirror how deals really move from first contact to closed. Instead of copying a generic sales pipeline template into a spreadsheet, you describe your sales process in plain language and the skill proposes a named funnel structure, confirms the stage names with you, and only then stands up the pipeline in your CRM. Drop the SKILL.md into Claude, Cursor, or Codex, and you get a repeatable way to design, review, and launch sales funnel stages without guesswork, dashboards, or a monthly subscription.

What you gain
  • A ready-to-use sales pipeline with logically ordered stages, not a blank spreadsheet
  • Stage names that match how your team actually sells, so nothing gets misfiled
  • A preview and approval step before anything is created, so you stay in control
  • Faster CRM setup: go from an idea to a working funnel in minutes, not hours
  • A consistent pipeline structure you can reuse for new products, regions, or teams
  • Clear visibility into where every deal sits, from new lead to closed
How it works, step by step
  • 1
    Drop the skill into your assistant

    Add the SKILL.md file to Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. There is nothing to install and no API key to design the pipeline.

  • 2
    Describe your sales process

    Tell it how you sell in plain language: how leads come in, when you send a proposal, how you close. The skill turns that into a proposed pipeline.

  • 3
    Review the proposed stages

    You get a named pipeline with ordered stages (for example: New Lead, In Progress, Meeting Booked, Proposal Sent, Contract, Closed). Rename, reorder, add, or remove any stage.

  • 4
    Approve before anything is created

    The skill always shows you the full pipeline name and stage list and waits for your explicit go-ahead. Nothing is created in your CRM until you confirm.

  • 5
    Connect your CRM when ready

    When you approve, point it at your own CRM account. It creates the pipeline and stages exactly as reviewed, with no surprise changes.

  • 6
    Get your working pipeline and next steps

    You receive a confirmed, live pipeline plus a plain summary of the stages so your team knows how to use it from day one.

What it generates
  • A named sales pipeline with ordered, ready-to-use stages
  • A clear list of CRM pipeline stages mapped to your real sales journey
  • A preview you approve before anything goes live in your CRM
  • A plain-language summary of what each stage means and when to move a deal
  • A reusable pipeline structure you can apply to new offers or teams
Variations

New product or service line

Spin up a separate pipeline with its own stages when a new offer needs a different sales journey than your main funnel.

Refine an existing funnel

Use it to rethink and rename stages so your pipeline reflects how your team truly sells, before you rebuild it in the CRM.

Team or region rollout

Reuse a proven stage structure to stand up matching pipelines for a new team, territory, or market quickly and consistently.

FAQ
What is the Create Pipeline skill?

It is a SKILL.md file you add to your AI assistant that helps you design a new sales pipeline with clear, ordered CRM stages and then set it up in your CRM after you approve it.

Does it work with Claude, Cursor, and Codex?

Yes. It works with any AI assistant that reads a SKILL.md file, including Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.

Do I need to install anything or pay a subscription?

No. There is nothing to install, no separate app, and no monthly subscription. You only connect your own CRM account when you are ready to create the pipeline.

Will it change my CRM without asking?

No. The skill always previews the pipeline name and full stage list and waits for your explicit approval. It only creates the pipeline once you confirm, so you stay in control.

How is this different from a sales pipeline template?

A template is a static file you fill in by hand. This skill designs stages around your actual sales process, checks the names with you, and builds the pipeline directly in your CRM.

Do I need design or coding skills to use it?

No. You describe your sales process in plain language. The skill handles the structure, the stage naming, and the setup for you.
