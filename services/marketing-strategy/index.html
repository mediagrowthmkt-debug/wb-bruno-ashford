The problem

Why more ad spend stops producing more customers

Campaigns get more expensive, the agency reports on clicks and impressions, and no one can say what a customer actually costs or whether the next dollar is worth spending. That is how marketing becomes a line item leadership wants to cut.

A strong marketing strategy fixes the economics first. When you know your cost to acquire a customer and the value they return, paid traffic stops being a gamble and becomes a dial you can turn with confidence.

Google Ads that reports on revenue, not clicks

Bruno Ashford connects the funnel end to end, from the ad to the qualified lead to the closed deal, so every campaign is judged on the number that pays the bills.

The Google Ads work is engineered around unit economics and reinforced with AI for testing and optimization, so budget flows to what converts and away from what doesn't.

FAQ

Questions executives ask first

What does a marketing strategy start with?

With your unit economics, cost to acquire a customer and the value they return, not with clicks. From there it architects paid traffic and Google Ads to buy qualified demand efficiently and reports on revenue, so spend turns into measurable growth.

How is this different from an agency?

Many agencies report on impressions and clicks because they are easy to show. This work ties the campaign to closed revenue, structures the account around your true cost per acquisition, and moves budget to what actually converts.

How do you measure whether paid traffic is working?

By connecting the full funnel, from the ad to the qualified lead to the closed deal, and judging every campaign against cost per acquisition and customer value. If a dollar of spend can't be traced to revenue, it doesn't get to keep spending.

Do you manage the ad accounts or just advise?

Both are on the table. The engagement can be strategic, building the model and the plan, or hands-on, architecting and optimizing the Google Ads and paid campaigns directly, with AI-assisted testing to speed up what's working.

Do you work with US and UK ad accounts?

Yes. Marketing strategy and paid traffic work is delivered in English for companies across the United States and the United Kingdom, with campaigns tuned to each market.
