The problem

Why your pipeline feels busy but revenue stays flat

Activity is not the constraint anymore. Teams send more emails, book more calls, and run more sequences, yet the forecast still swings on a handful of deals and a lot of optimism.

Sales consulting fixes the system underneath the activity: where deals stall, why good leads go cold, and which two stages of the cycle actually decide the outcome. Get that right and volume stops being the answer, signal does.

B2B sales process consulting that sticks

As a b2b sales consultant, Bruno Ashford rebuilds your sales process around how buyers actually decide, then instruments it so you can see where revenue leaks and coach to it.

The sales process consulting work pairs a clean, repeatable pipeline with AI for research and follow-up, so reps walk into every conversation prepared and no deal dies in the gap between meetings.

FAQ

Questions executives ask first

What does a sales consultant do?

A sales consultant diagnoses why revenue is unpredictable and rebuilds the commercial system behind it. That means fixing where deals stall, defining a repeatable process any rep can run, and wiring in AI for research and follow-up, so the forecast stops swinging on a few heroic deals.

What is sales process consulting?

Sales process consulting maps your pipeline stage by stage, finds the two moments where deals actually get won or lost, and rebuilds the motion around how buyers decide. The goal is a repeatable, measurable process, not a motivational session that fades in a week.

Do you help B2B sales teams specifically?

Yes. This is B2B sales consultant work at its core, longer cycles, multiple stakeholders, and higher deal values, where a disciplined process and AI-assisted prep and follow-up produce the biggest lift.

Will this work if we don't have clean CRM data?

Part of the engagement is getting your data honest before automating anything. We instrument the pipeline first, because you cannot trust a forecast, or an AI, on a stage you cannot measure.

How does AI fit into sales consulting?

AI concentrates its return in three places: research and prep, follow-up and continuity, and forecasting and coaching. We deploy it exactly there and keep a human in the room for the moments that decide the deal.
